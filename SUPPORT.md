# Getting help

- **Questions and setup help:** ask in the [Uptimy Discord](https://www.upti.my/discord), in the **#help** forum.
- **Bugs and feature requests:** for Uptimy, in [feedback](https://github.com/uptimy/feedback/issues/new/choose). For [agent](https://github.com/uptimy/agent/issues/new/choose), [uptimyctl](https://github.com/uptimy/uptimyctl/issues/new/choose) or the [MCP server](https://github.com/uptimy/mcp/issues/new/choose), in their own repositories.
- **Security issues:** report them privately, see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Uptimy account or billing:** [support@upti.my](mailto:support@upti.my).

Docs: [www.upti.my/docs](https://www.upti.my/docs).
